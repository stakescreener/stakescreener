StakeScreener Safe Manager

SAFE Manager

Works with Staker app smart accounts (Safe 1.3.0) and any Safe with a threshold of 1.

For HEX stakes held in a Safe contract. You can end stakes, mint HDRN and COM, and send funds out of the Safe. Every action is simulated first, and nothing is sent until you confirm it in your own wallet.

Activity

How it works. A Safe is a contract wallet. With a threshold of 1, any single owner can execute a transaction for it. This page builds the call (for example stakeEnd on the HEX contract) and wraps it in the Safe's execTransaction, signed by your connection as an owner. It then simulates the transaction and only after that asks your wallet to send it. Safes that need more than one signature are shown read-only. For those, use Safe{Wallet}.

Emergency end (EES) ends a stake before its end date. HEX takes a penalty, and the page shows the exact amount first. That calculation follows the HEX contract and was verified to the last heart against 8 real early ends. Ending on time has no penalty. Ending more than 14 days late does have one.

Read-only parts use public RPCs. There is no tracking and no WalletConnect or other third-party wallet SDK, only the wallet in your browser (MetaMask, Rabby, OKX; a Ledger works through them). The source code is public and covered by Verify this site. Use at your own risk. Informational tool, not financial advice.

Disclaimer
  • You sign every transaction yourself. This page never holds keys or funds and cannot move anything on its own. It only prepares a transaction and hands it to the wallet in your browser; nothing happens until you confirm it there.
  • Transactions are final. An ended stake cannot be restarted and a transfer cannot be reversed. Check the action, the amount and the recipient before you confirm, also on your hardware wallet's screen.
  • Emergency end (EES) ends a stake before its end date, and the HEX contract takes a penalty that can be a large part of the stake or all of the yield. The amount shown is calculated from the HEX data up to now; the contract applies the exact rules on the day the transaction is mined.
  • A simulation is not a guarantee. Each action is checked with eth_call before sending, but the blockchain can change between the simulation and the moment your transaction is mined.
  • Safes with a threshold of 1: any single owner can end stakes and move everything the Safe holds. Make sure you know and control every owner listed.
  • Values, balances and HDRN / COM amounts are read from public RPC nodes and can be wrong, delayed or missing. Hedron and Communis are third-party protocols; their contracts decide what is minted.
  • StakeScreener is not affiliated with Staker app, Safe, HEX, Hedron or Communis.
  • Nothing on this site is financial, investment, tax or legal advice. The tool is open source and provided “as is”, without any warranty. Use it at your own risk.
Privacy
  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, and nothing is stored in your browser. The Safe address stays in the page address (after #), which is never sent to our server.
  • To read the Safe, your browser contacts public RPC nodes directly (PublicNode, PulseChain, dRPC). They see your IP address and the addresses you look up, under their own privacy policies.
  • Your wallet address becomes visible to this page only after you click “Connect wallet”. You can disconnect the site in your wallet at any time.
